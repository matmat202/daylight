      The year

      Sun times are computed in your browser with NOAA's solar-position equations: sunrise and sunset are when the top of the sun crosses a flat horizon, with the standard allowance for refraction. Checked against the US Naval Observatory to the minute for Palo Alto, London, Reykjavík, Sydney, Singapore, Tromsø and Apia. Moonrise, moonset and the moon's phase come from the main terms of Meeus' lunar series, checked against the Naval Observatory to the minute in seven towns. Hills, buildings and altitude can move what you see by a few minutes. Towns: GeoNames (CC BY 4.0), every place of 15,000+ people, with its time zone. Nothing leaves your browser.
